About Us

One of Johannesburg's leading building & construction companies

Over the years Tyan Construction has grown to become one of the leading building and construction companies in Johannesburg. Originally formed in 1999 by Tyrone Gerber, the business has steadily progressed and expanded into the dynamic company it is today. With a staff complement of 55 employees, our highly qualified team is on hand to tackle all your general building requirements.

In its earlier years, Tyan Construction concentrated on remodeling and building KFC franchises — 198 KFC stores delivered with great success. That became a stepping stone into general building, and taking over an existing shop-fitting business let us fold many more trades into our services.

In 2005 we acquired our own 4 800m² workshop and office in Booysens, Johannesburg, giving our clients a genuine one-stop-shop experience — electrical work, ceilings, partitions, shop fittings and general building, all under one roof.

“We are all about quality workmanship, delivered in a manner that is professional, affordable, reliable and efficient.” — The Tyan Commitment
