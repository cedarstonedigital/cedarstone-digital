Intake

Tell us what happened.

You do not need to be certain it was discrimination. You do not need a lawyer, a document, or the right words for it. Working out whether something unlawful happened is our job.

If you would rather speak to a person, call 202.234.3062 or 866.549.0009, or email info@equalrightscenter.org. Intake is free.
1. What happened

Pick the closest match. If none fit, choose somewhere else.

A landlord, letting agent, employer or business name if you know it. An address is fine too.

An approximate date is fine. Time limits apply to some complaints, so earlier is better.

Write it however it comes out. There is no format we need.

2. How to reach you

We use this to follow up. Give a phone number below if email is difficult.

For example a preferred format, an interpreter, a screen reader, or a safe time to call.

What you send stays with the ERC intake team. Filing here does not replace your right to complain directly to the US Department of Housing and Urban Development, the DC Office of Human Rights, or your county human rights office, and it does not close off that route.

What happens next

Three steps, and none of them cost you anything.

01

We read it

An intake coordinator goes through what you sent and comes back to you, usually with a few questions.

02

We look into it

Depending on what you describe, that can mean testing the same provider, helping you prepare a request, or helping you file.

03

We tell you where it stands

Including when the answer is that there is not enough to act on. You will not be left wondering.

04

Patterns get acted on

One account can become part of something larger. Several matching accounts are what moves a case forward.
