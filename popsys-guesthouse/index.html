About Us

Self-Catering Comfort in the Heart of the City

Popsy's Guesthouse is a recently renovated 3-star self-catering accommodation nestled at the vibrant heart of Johannesburg's CBD. We offer a true home-away-from-home experience — thoughtfully furnished rooms, a warm atmosphere, and everything you need for a comfortable stay.

Just steps from the Museum of African Design, Arts on Main, and the Carlton Centre, we are perfectly positioned for both business and leisure travellers. With 8 beautifully appointed rooms, free WiFi, private parking, and round-the-clock security — your comfort and safety always come first.
