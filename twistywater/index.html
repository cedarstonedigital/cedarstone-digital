Why Twisty

Water with a twist

TwistyWater started with a simple complaint: plain water gets boring, and most flavoured drinks are just sugar in disguise. So we make still spring water with a real infusion of fruit or herbs and nothing else. No sugar, no sweeteners, no colourants.

Four flavours, one promise: if it is not something you would happily drink all day, it does not go in the can.

Good questions

Is TwistyWater still or sparkling?

Still. It is spring water with a natural infusion, so it drinks like water, not like a soft drink.

Does it contain sugar or sweeteners?

No. Zero sugar, zero sweeteners, zero calories. The flavour comes entirely from the fruit and herb infusion.

What exactly is in the can?

Still spring water and a natural infusion of about one percent. No preservatives and no colourants. Each flavour lists its exact ingredients on this site and on the can.

Are the cans recyclable?

Yes. Aluminium can be recycled over and over without losing quality, so please give the can a quick rinse and recycle it.

How should I store it?

Anywhere cool works, but TwistyWater is at its best ice cold. Keep a few in the fridge.
