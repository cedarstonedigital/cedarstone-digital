The House

Started in 2022.
Built on the cars
nobody else kept.

Pupil of Fate opened on Sheikh Zayed Road with a straightforward idea: treat 1980s and 1990s collector cars with the same seriousness the market reserves for current hypercars — and be honest about the condition of both.

The facility runs to 140,000 square feet across trading, rental and a dedicated workshop. We inspect every car ourselves before it reaches the floor, we publish the odometer, and we have never refused an independent inspection.
