Our Story

A Little Bit of Ohana

Ohana means family — and that is more or less how the place runs.

We are a small café on Main Road in Kalk Bay, close enough to the water that you can hear it over the coffee machine. People come in after a swim at the tidal pool, after the market, after a long walk along the harbour — and stay a lot longer than they meant to.

The food is made fresh, in a small kitchen, by people who actually eat it. There is always something plant-based on the pass, always a cake under the dome, and always a table where somebody is on their third cappuccino with no intention of leaving. That is the idea.

Pull up a chair. You are family here.

Dietary

Something For Everyone

Ohana has always leaned plant-forward. There are vegan-friendly dishes on every part of the menu, and non-dairy milk across the whole coffee list.

Vegetarians are spoilt for choice, and the kitchen will happily talk through what can be adapted. If you have a specific allergy or requirement, mention it when you order — or call ahead and we'll tell you honestly what works.

Vegan options Vegetarian options Gluten-friendly options Non-dairy milks

These are options on the menu, not the whole menu — Ohana is not an exclusively vegan or gluten-free kitchen, and it isn't an allergen-free environment. Please tell us what you need and we'll be straight with you.
