The kitchen

Cooked to order,
never to a clock

Chef Thabiso Nkosi and a brigade of eleven work an open pass three metres from the dining room. Nothing is held under a lamp. If a plate is not right, it does not leave — which is why we ask for a little patience on a full Saturday, and why it is worth it.

Menus change with the season and with what our growers in the Midlands and the Cape can actually deliver that week.
