The grill

Fire, patience, precision

Beef is aged twenty-eight days on the premises, seasoned an hour before service and rested as long as it is cooked. There is no faster way to do it properly.

From the cellar

What to drink
with all of this

Fillet or lamb — a Stellenbosch cabernet with a few years on it, or the Swartland syrah if you like a little pepper.

Salmon or risotto — Hemel-en-Aarde chardonnay, or the Elgin pinot noir served lightly chilled.

Duck — ask for the Piedmont nebbiolo; it is the best forty rand you will spend on this menu.

A three-glass pairing flight is R210 per person and can be added to any main when you order.
